# Auto-Fishing (Fabric)

A simple and lightweight Minecraft mod designed for AFK auto-fishing.

## Requirements

1. **Fabric Loader** installed for 26.3 Minecraft version.
2. **Fabric API** (`fabric-api-0.162.0+26.3.jar` or a compatible version) placed in your `mods` folder.

---

## How to Use

1. Equip your **Fishing Rod**.
2. Stand near a body of water and aim at it.
3. Press the **`J`** key to toggle auto-fishing:
   * **GREEN message:** Auto-fishing enabled.
   * **RED message:** Auto-fishing disabled.
4. **To Stop:** Press **`J`** again or simply unequip your fishing rod.
